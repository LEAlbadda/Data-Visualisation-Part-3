Research question: Which Dubai communities account for the most property sales, and how does off-plan reliance differ between them?

Each rectangle is a community, grouped by its Dubai sector. Select a sector to zoom in and compare its communities. Select “All sectors” to return to the full view.

Encoding

Rectangle area encodes the number of qualifying sales in each community. Colour uses a sequential blue scale to encode the percentage of sales registered as off-plan. The hierarchy groups communities by sector.

Interaction and value added

Click a sector to expand its communities; use “All sectors” to return. Hover over any rectangle for the community name, sales count, off-plan share, existing-sales share and median price per m². This reveals both transaction concentration and the balance between off-plan and existing sales.

Data: Dubai Land Department transactions supplied for CS333; community-level aggregation in community_data.js. Sales are filtered to 2015–2026 with meter_sale_price greater than zero.
